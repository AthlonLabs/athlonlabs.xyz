Privacy Policy for Cascade TD

Effective date: October 26, 2026
Published by: Athlon Labs, LLC
Contact: support@athlonlabs.xyz

The short version

Cascade TD has no accounts and no ads, and it does not track you. To balance the game, the app sends anonymous gameplay data from each run to our own server, hosted by Cloudflare. That data carries no identifier and is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or Game Center profile. Your progress and saved runs stay on your device.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves these things on your device so the game works:

  • Settings. Your sound, colorblind mode, reduce motion, fast-forward and tutorial choices.
  • Progress. Maps you have cleared, your best scores, tutorial progress and which enemies you have seen.
  • Saved runs. A snapshot of a run in progress, so you can resume it.

This data stays on your device. We cannot see it.

What we collect, and why

Gameplay data, to balance the game. Which maps you play, which towers you build and upgrade, where you place them, which wave you reach, and when you lose lives.

Each run is sent once, as a list of game events, to a server we run on Cloudflare's network (Cloudflare, Inc. hosts it for us). The upload carries a random number made specifically for that run and the day it was played, never the time of day, so two runs cannot be connected to each other or to you. Our server does not store your IP address; it uses a scrambled form, temporarily, to prevent abuse. We do not send your name, email, location, Apple ID, Game Center identity, advertising identifier or any device identifier. We use this data only to balance and improve the game. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Ladder seasons. The app checks our server for the current Ladder season, which says which challenge and leaderboard to use. That request sends nothing about you or your play, and our server does not keep your IP address.

Crash reports and performance. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us crash reports and performance figures for Cascade TD, such as launch time, hangs, memory and battery use. Apple controls that setting, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings, Privacy and Security, Analytics and Improvements.

How long we keep it

Gameplay data is kept for up to 24 months and then deleted automatically.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your Ladder scores are submitted to that season's Game Center leaderboard. Apple runs Game Center and shows your score under your Game Center profile; Apple's privacy policy covers that. If you are not signed in, Ladder still works and keeps your best score for each season on your device. We do not receive your Game Center account details.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or advertising identifier. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it contains no advertising SDKs.

Children

Cascade TD is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and deleting your data

All of your progress is stored on your device. To remove it, delete the app. Device backups (for example iCloud) may keep a copy until you delete those backups through your Apple account.

Gameplay data carries no identifier, so we cannot find or separate the records that came from you. If you do not agree, or no longer wish to share this data, you must delete the app. Records are deleted at the end of the retention period above.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, there is usually nothing for us to retrieve, but we will answer any request sent to the contact address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date.
